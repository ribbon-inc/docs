## A few helpful places to start

- **Creating your first role:** Build an interview that helps you learn what matters for your team.
- **Setting up automated outreach:** Invite candidates when they reach the right stage in your ATS.
- **Using Multi-Req:** Bring multiple requisitions or locations together under one role.
- **Sharing a shortlist:** Give others an easy way to review selected candidates.
