---
title: "Request an interview accommodation"
description: "How to ask for support or an alternative format if you need one."
---

We want you to feel comfortable and supported during your interview.

If you need an accommodation due to a disability, submit a request before starting your interview. Your recruiter will be notified, and Ribbon's Customer Success team will work with them to help arrange a suitable option.

## What accommodations are available?

Accommodations may include adjustments to the interview experience or an alternative format, such as an interview with a member of the hiring team.

## Before you continue

If you see a consent checkbox linking to the employer's privacy or accessibility policy, review it before continuing.

## Who can help?

- **Accommodation requests or alternative arrangements:** Contact your recruiter or hiring team.
- **Technical or accessibility issues using Ribbon:** Email [support@ribbon.ai](mailto:support@ribbon.ai). We're here to help.
