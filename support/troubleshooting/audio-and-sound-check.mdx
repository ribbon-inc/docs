---
title: "Troubleshoot audio and sound check issues"
description: "Fix problems hearing the interviewer or being heard."
---

If you can't hear the interviewer or your microphone isn't working, try these steps. Start with the quick checks below.

## Start with a few quick checks

- **Check Bluetooth:** Disconnect any headphones or speakers you aren't using. Your device may be sending audio to them.
- **Turn up your volume:** On your phone, check the **media volume**, which may be separate from your ringtone volume.
- **Try the speaker test:** Reopen your interview link and select **Test Speaker**.

## Allow sound in your browser

If you're using **Chrome on Android**:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open settings">
    Tap the **three dots (⋮)** → **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open sound settings">
    Open **Site settings** → **Sound**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow sound">
    Make sure sites are allowed to play sound.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check microphone permissions

Ribbon needs microphone access for your interview. When prompted, choose **Allow**.

If you previously blocked access, open the site controls beside the address bar and check **Permissions**. Allow **Microphone** access and, if shown, **Sound**, then refresh the page.

## Check your connection

- Temporarily turn off your **VPN** or **Data Saver**, if enabled and you're able to.
- If you're using a work device or office Wi-Fi, try a personal device or another connection, such as mobile data.

For more help, see [Troubleshoot network and connection issues](/troubleshooting/network-and-connection).

## Restart your browser

Close the browser completely, then reopen your interview link and try **Test Speaker** again.

## Clear cache and cookies

In Chrome, go to **Settings** → **Privacy and security** → **Delete browsing data**. Select **Cookies and site data** and **Cached images and files**, then delete the data and reopen your interview link.

<Note>
  Clearing cookies may sign you out of other websites.
</Note>

## Try a desktop or laptop

If you're still having trouble on a phone or tablet, open your interview link on a **desktop or laptop**.
