---
title: "Expired interview links"
description: "What to do if your link says the role is closed."
---

If you open your interview link and see that the role is **closed** or **no longer accepting interviews**, it usually means the company has stopped accepting interviews for that position.

## What should I do?

The best next step is to reach out to the recruiter or hiring team that sent your invitation. They can let you know whether you can still interview and help you with next steps. When you get in touch, share your interview link and the position you're applying for.

Ribbon provides the AI-powered interview software, while the company manages the role and interview invitations. Although we can't reset the link or reopen the role ourselves, their hiring team can help clarify your options.
