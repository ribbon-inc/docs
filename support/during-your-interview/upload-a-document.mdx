---
title: "Upload a document for your interview"
description: "How to add a resume or other file during your interview."
---

You may be asked to upload a document, such as a resume, as part of your interview. Here's how to add your file.

## Before you upload

Make sure your document is saved as a **PDF or DOCX file** and is **under 10 MB**.

## How to upload your document

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the upload option">
    When you reach the document upload step, select the upload option.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your file">
    Choose the file you'd like to upload from your device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue">
    Wait for your file to finish uploading, then click **Next** to continue.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Can I skip this step?

If uploading is optional, select **Next** to continue without a file. If it's required, you'll need to upload one before continuing.

## Having trouble uploading?

- **Check the format:** Save or export your document as a PDF or DOCX.
- **Reduce the size if needed:** Compress the file or remove large images.
- **Try again:** Check your internet connection, then retry the upload. If needed, try another browser or device.

Still having trouble? Email [support@ribbon.ai](mailto:support@ribbon.ai) with the error message, your file type and size, and what you've tried. We're happy to help.
