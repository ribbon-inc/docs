## What is a Ribbon interview?

Your Ribbon interview is a conversation with an AI interviewer. You'll answer questions aloud, much like a phone call.

You'll see a simple interview screen with basic controls. There's no AI avatar and no screen sharing. If the hiring team requires video, you'll also see your own camera feed.

## When can I take it?

Open the link in your interview invitation when you're ready. There's no need to book a time. Check your invitation for any deadline or instructions from the hiring team.

## How long will it take?

Check the estimated duration shown before you start, and set aside enough time to finish without interruptions. The length may vary depending on your answers.

## Before you start

- **Choose a quiet space** where you can speak comfortably.
- **Use Google Chrome on a desktop or laptop** for the smoothest experience. If you opened the link inside an app, such as Indeed, copy it into Chrome.
- **Check your internet connection** and allow microphone access when prompted. If video is required, allow camera access too.
- **Complete the Sound Check** before starting. This checks that you can hear the interviewer and that your microphone is working.

## What should I expect during the interview?

Listen to each question and answer in your own words. Take your time and share examples from your experience. The interviewer may ask follow-up questions for more detail.
