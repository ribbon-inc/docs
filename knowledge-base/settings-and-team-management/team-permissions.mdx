---
title: "Team roles and permissions"
description: "Manage who can access roles, candidates, and settings."
---

Ribbon offers three common permission levels:

- **Member:** Work with assigned roles and review candidates.
- **Team Admin:** Manage members in their sub-team and import requisitions.
- **System Admin:** Manage organization-wide settings, ATS connections, billing, and users.

## Update a teammate’s role

1. Go to **Settings → Teams and Members**.
2. Find the person in **Directory**.
3. Use the **Seat** dropdown to change their role.

## Inviting teammates

Use **Settings → Teams and Members** to invite users. If invitations don’t arrive, ask IT to allow emails from `ribbon.ai`. For SSO, IT may need to assign the user to Ribbon in the identity provider.

If an invite fails, log out, clear Ribbon cookies, and reopen the link. Contact [support@ribbon.ai](mailto:support@ribbon.ai) if you’re still stuck.
