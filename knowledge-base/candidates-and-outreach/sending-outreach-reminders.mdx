Invite candidates through email, SMS, or WhatsApp without sending each message individually. Customize invitations and reminders to keep next steps clear.

## Before you begin

Make sure candidates have been added to the role. See [Adding candidates](/candidates/adding-candidates).

Email outreach needs an email address. SMS and WhatsApp need a phone number. Candidates must consent to SMS and can opt out of recruiting texts and reminders.

## Send outreach

1. Open the role and click **Send outreach**, or select candidates and choose **Bulk actions → Send outreach**.
2. Use filters and select the candidates you'd like to contact.
3. Choose **email**, **SMS**, **WhatsApp**, or a combination.
4. Customize the messages, including your company, role, expected interview length, and deadline.
5. Set message timing and reminders.
6. Review previews and recipients, then send.

## Check your outreach

Open the role's **Outreach** tab to review messages, channels, recipient counts, and send dates. **Not sent yet** means outreach is set up but hasn't been sent.

The candidate list separates **Added**, **Invited**, **Completed interviews**, and **Incomplete interview attempts** to help you see who may need attention.

## Automate invitations through your ATS

You can also invite candidates automatically when they reach a selected ATS stage. See [Setting up outreach automation](https://app.mintlify.com/ribbon-198dd1e6/ribbon-198dd1e6/~/Setting-up-outreach-automation-8c64431f-0799-4c03-b862-10cb097ea4ee).

Candidates already at that stage when automation is enabled need manual outreach.
