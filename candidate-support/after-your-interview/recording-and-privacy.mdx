Here's what to know about recording and privacy when you interview through Ribbon.

## Is my interview recorded?

Yes. Ribbon records and transcribes interviews, which may include audio and video. This helps the hiring team review your responses.

## How is my interview information used?

Ribbon uses your responses to generate transcripts, summaries, and insights to support the hiring team's review. Hiring decisions remain with the company you're applying to.

## Have a privacy question?

You're welcome to reach out to [privacy@ribbon.ai](mailto:privacy@ribbon.ai) with questions about your interview data. For full details on how information is collected, used, shared, and retained, along with your privacy rights, please visit our [Privacy Policy](https://www.ribbon.ai/privacy-policy).
