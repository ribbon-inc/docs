---
description: "Find quick answers to common questions about hiring with Ribbon."
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Ribbon?">
    Ribbon is an AI-powered interview platform that helps your team learn more about candidates before moving them to the next stage. Candidates complete a voice interview on their own time, and you can review their responses, summaries, and scores afterward.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I create a role?">
    Go to **Roles** and follow the prompts to create a role, add your interview questions, and configure your settings. If your organization has an ATS integration, you can also create a role from your ATS.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I invite candidates to an interview?">
    You can share the role’s interview link or send outreach directly from Ribbon.

    To send outreach, select the candidates, open **Bulk actions**, and choose **Send outreach**. Follow the prompts to choose your outreach channel and send your invitations.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does adding a candidate automatically send an invitation?">
    Adding a candidate to Ribbon doesn’t automatically send an interview invitation. You’ll need to send outreach or have an outreach automation configured.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I automate interview invitations through my ATS?">
    If your organization has an ATS integration, you can configure outreach to send when a candidate enters a selected stage.

    Candidates already in that stage when you enable the automation won’t be invited automatically. You can send outreach to those candidates manually.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use one interview for multiple requisitions or locations?">
    Yes! Multi-Req lets you link multiple requisitions or locations under one Ribbon role, so your team can use the same interview and review candidates in one place.

    Open the role, go to **Settings → ATS**, and click **Link a requisition**. Search for and select the requisitions you’d like to include, then click **Link**. You can link requisitions that haven’t been added to Ribbon yet, as well as existing active Ribbon roles.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do candidates need to schedule their interviews?">
    Candidates can complete their interviews whenever works best for them, before any deadline you’ve set. There’s no need to coordinate a time with your team.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I require candidates to turn on their camera?">
    Yes. Enable **Require Video** in the role’s interview settings to require candidates to use their camera. They’ll be prompted to enable it before starting.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if a candidate’s interview is interrupted?">
    Candidates have up to one hour to return through their original interview link and resume.

    After that window, the interview is submitted with the responses they completed, and your team can see that it’s incomplete.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Who handles requests to restart or retake an interview?">
    Your hiring team handles interview reset requests. Candidates should contact you directly if they need another attempt. Ribbon support can help troubleshoot technical issues.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where can I review completed interviews?">
    Open the role and select a candidate to review their interview summary, scores, and transcript. Video is also available for interviews recorded with video enabled.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I share candidates with someone outside Ribbon?">
    Yes! **Shortlists** let you share selected candidates from any role with someone outside your Ribbon account.

    Anyone with the link can view the shortlist for 180 days, including candidate summaries and scorecards. Keep this in mind when choosing who to share the link with.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where can I check my plan and interview usage?">
    Go to **Organization settings → Plan & Usage** to review your subscription and usage. For billing options, select **Manage Billing**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I report a bug or request a feature?">
    Open the support messenger in Ribbon and let us know what you’re experiencing or what you’d like to see.

    For a technical issue, include the affected role or candidate link, what happened, and any screenshots or error messages. For a feature request, tell us what you’re trying to accomplish and how it would help your team.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
